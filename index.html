1. Pipeline Architecture & Project Purpose

Clinical Parkinson's Disease (PD) diagnosis currently relies on motor symptoms that emerge only after loss of >60% of substantia nigra dopaminergic neurons. This computational framework isolates objective, non-redundant transcriptomic biomarkers from prefrontal cortex RNA-Seq profiles to elucidate molecular pathology and support early biomarker development.

2. The 5 Machine Learning Methods Explained

Each algorithm leverages distinct mathematical principles to evaluate candidate genes:

1. LASSO (L1 Regularization)

Sparse Linear

Adds an \( \ell_1 \) penalty term \( \lambda \sum |\beta_j| \) to logistic regression loss. Highly correlated redundant genes have their coefficients driven to exactly 0, isolating an independent linear signature.

Strengths: Prevents collinear redundancy. Limitation: Discards non-linear interactions.

2. Boruta (Random Forest Shadows)

All-Relevant

Creates permuted "shadow features" and iteratively trains Random Forests over 200 iterations. Genes whose Z-scores significantly exceed the maximal shadow Z-score are statistically confirmed.

Strengths: Finds all biologically relevant features. Limitation: Computationally intensive.

3. SVM-RFE

Margin Maximization

Trains a linear Support Vector Machine to maximize the separation margin between PD and Control samples. Recursively prunes the genes with the smallest squared weights \( w_j^2 \).

Strengths: Robust against high-dimensional noise. Limitation: Greedy backward elimination.

4. XGBoost

Gradient Boosting

Builds an ensemble of gradient-boosted decision trees optimizing second-order Taylor expansion loss. Genes are ranked by total Gain (improvement in accuracy brought by splits on that feature).

Strengths: Uncovers complex non-linear epistatic effects. Limitation: Susceptible to overfitting small cohorts.

5. Mutual Information

Information Theoretic

Computes \( I(X; Y) = \iint p(x, y) \log \frac{p(x, y)}{p(x)p(y)} dx dy \) via k-nearest neighbor entropy estimation. Measures the reduction in uncertainty of clinical diagnosis given the expression level of the gene without assuming linearity.

Strengths: Model-agnostic and captures arbitrary non-linear dependencies. Limitation: Univariate (does not assess gene-gene interactions).

3. Consensus Rule Logic

The pipeline synthesizes multi-algorithm outputs through a deterministic 4-case decision hierarchy:

Condition Rule Identifier Decision Strategy Rationale
Only 1 method selected single_method Returns top-10 ranked genes from that single algorithm directly. No consensus required.
≥ 2 methods & intersection ≠ ∅ strict_intersection Retains only genes selected by 100% of the active methods. Highest possible cross-algorithm confidence.
≥ 2 methods & intersection = ∅ consensus_majority Retains genes agreed upon by ≥ 2 methods, sorted by vote count. Graceful relaxation to preserve multi-algorithm support.
All overlap is empty fallback_top1 Takes the rank-1 gene from each active algorithm. Guarantees a valid non-empty evaluation panel.

4. Diagnostic Plots Interpretation Guide

How hiring managers, clinicians, and researchers should read each diagnostic visualization:

📈 ROC Curve & AUC:

Plots Sensitivity vs (1 - Specificity) across every probability threshold. An AUC of 0.50 denotes coin-toss chance, while >0.80 denotes strong discrimination. Our consensus panel achieves 0.912.

🎯 Confusion Matrix:

Quantifies real-world classification errors in postmortem samples. Shows that false negatives (5) and false positives (5) remain minimal across 72 clinical specimens.

📊 Biomarker Boxplots:

Exhibits biological effect size. For instance, ADAM33 shows consistent upregulation in PD brain tissue, providing a verifiable molecular signal.

⚖️ SHAP Importance:

Shapley values from cooperative game theory attribute exact credit to each biomarker for shifting predictions toward Parkinson's Disease.

5. Dataset & Provenance (GSE68719)

The discovery cohort is sourced from NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus accession GSE68719. The study profiled postmortem human brain prefrontal cortex (Brodmann Area 9) from neuropathologically verified Parkinson's Disease donors (N=28) and neurologically intact healthy controls (N=44).

Sequencing was conducted using the Illumina HiSeq 2000 platform (GPL11154). Raw counts were normalized and batch-inspected prior to differential expression modeling.
